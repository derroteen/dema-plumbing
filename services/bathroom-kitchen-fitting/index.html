What We Install

Bathroom & Kitchen Fitting Services

We handle every plumbing element of your bathroom or kitchen project — from supply pipes through to final fixture installation and waterproofing.

Bathroom Fittings

Toilet installation, vanity basin mounting, shower enclosures, bathtub fitting, towel rail plumbing, and waterproofing — all professionally sealed and tested.

Kitchen Plumbing

Kitchen sink installation, mixer tap fitting, dishwasher connections, water filtration systems, and waste disposal unit plumbing.

Waterproofing & Sealing

Proper wet-area waterproofing for showers and baths — preventing leaks into floors, walls, and downstairs ceilings before they start.

Full Renovations

We work with your builder and tiler to handle all plumbing elements of a full bathroom or kitchen renovation from demolition to final installation.

Common Questions

Fitting FAQs

Can you supply the fixtures or do I buy them myself?

Either works. We can source quality fixtures at trade prices, or you can choose your own and we'll install them. We'll advise on compatibility and quality before purchase if needed.

How long does a full bathroom renovation take?

A complete bathroom strip-out and refit typically takes 3–7 days depending on scope. We'll give you a detailed timeline at the start of your project.

Do you coordinate with tilers and builders?

Yes. We're experienced working alongside other trades and can schedule our visits to fit around tiling, plastering, and electrical work.

Do you handle commercial kitchen plumbing?

Yes — commercial kitchens, restaurant fit-outs, and food preparation areas are something we do regularly. We ensure installations meet health and safety requirements.

What if I just need one fixture replaced?

No job is too small. We replace single taps, toilets, showers, or sinks without requiring a full renovation project.
