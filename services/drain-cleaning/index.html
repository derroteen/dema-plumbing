How We Work

Our Drain Cleaning Process

We use professional equipment to fully clear blockages — not just push them further down the line.

Camera Inspection

Before we start, we inspect the drain with a CCTV camera to pinpoint the exact blockage location and type — no guesswork.

Hydro-Jetting

High-pressure water jets blast through grease, scale, and debris — restoring full pipe diameter for commercial and domestic drains.

Mechanical Snaking

Common Questions

Drain Cleaning FAQs

How do I know if my drain needs professional cleaning?

Slow draining, gurgling sounds, bad odours from drains, or water backing up are all signs of a developing blockage. It's better to act early before a partial clog becomes a full blockage.

Can't I just use chemical drain cleaners?

Chemical cleaners damage pipes over time and rarely fully clear blockages. Professional hydro-jetting removes the entire clog and cleans the pipe walls — a far more effective and pipe-safe solution.

How long does drain cleaning take?

Most residential drain clears take 30–90 minutes. Main line or commercial drain jobs may take 2–4 hours depending on the extent of the blockage.

Do you service commercial properties?

Yes. We regularly service commercial kitchens, restaurants, office buildings, and apartment complexes. We can also set up scheduled maintenance contracts.

Will drain cleaning damage my pipes?

No — our equipment is calibrated for the pipe type and diameter. Hydro-jetting pressure is adjusted based on the pipe material to prevent damage.
