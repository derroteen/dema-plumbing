Common Questions

Emergency Plumbing FAQs

How quickly can you arrive for a plumbing emergency?

We aim to respond within 60 minutes for calls in Eldoret and surrounding areas. Response times to other regions may vary — call us and we'll give you an honest ETA immediately.

What should I do while waiting for your plumber?

Turn off the mains water stopcock to limit flooding. If it's an electrical hazard, turn off power at the fuse box. Don't attempt to repair burst or high-pressure pipes yourself.

Do you charge extra for night or weekend callouts?

We charge transparent emergency rates which we'll quote before any work begins. There are no hidden surcharges — the price we state is what you pay.

Can you perform a permanent repair on the same visit?

In most cases, yes. We carry common parts and materials on our emergency vehicles. Some complex repairs may require a return visit with specialist parts, but we will always secure the situation before leaving.

What areas do you cover for emergency callouts?

We cover Eldoret, Kisumu, Kisii, Homa Bay, Migori, Siaya, Nandi Hills, Kapsabet, and surrounding towns. Call us to confirm coverage for your specific location.
