How We Work

Our Leak Detection Process

State-of-the-art non-invasive equipment to pinpoint leaks quickly — no unnecessary digging or wall removal.

Thermal Imaging

Infrared cameras detect moisture and temperature differences behind walls, under floors, and in ceilings — invisible to the naked eye.

Acoustic Listening

Ground microphones amplify the sound of escaping water to locate the exact leak position within your pipe system.

Pressure Testing

We isolate pipe sections and test pressure integrity to confirm and map the full extent of any leak before repairs begin.

Durable Repairs

Certified plumbers repair pipes, faucets, slab leaks, and water mains using quality materials — with a full workmanship warranty.

Common Questions

Leak Detection FAQs

How do I know if I have a hidden leak?

Common signs: unexplained rises in your water bill, damp patches on walls or ceilings, low water pressure, sound of running water when all taps are off, or mould appearing in unusual areas.

Will you need to break walls to find the leak?

Rarely. Our thermal imaging and acoustic equipment locate leaks non-invasively in most cases. We only open walls when the confirmed repair location requires it — keeping disruption to a minimum.

How long does leak detection take?

A typical residential assessment takes 1–2 hours. Complex commercial properties or slab investigations may take longer. We give you a time estimate before starting.

Do you offer emergency leak detection?

Yes — 24/7. Call +254 721 342 738 at any time and our team will dispatch immediately.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

All repair work carries a workmanship warranty. If the same leak recurs due to our repair, we return and fix it at no cost.
