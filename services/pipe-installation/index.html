Common Questions

Pipe Installation FAQs

What type of pipe is best for my home?

Copper is highly durable and suited for hot and cold water. PEX is flexible, freeze-resistant, and cost-effective for residential installs. PVC is ideal for cold water and drainage lines. We'll recommend the best option for your specific situation and budget.

How long does a full repipe take?

A typical residential repipe takes 1–3 days depending on property size. We work methodically to minimise disruption to your water supply.

Do you handle commercial pipe installations?

Yes. We have experience with multi-storey commercial buildings, apartment complexes, and industrial facilities. We provide detailed plans and compliance documentation on request.

Will you repair the walls after pipe installation?

We keep wall openings to a minimum through careful planning. For major repiping, we coordinate with your builder or tiler for surface restoration. We always clean up fully after our work.

Is there a warranty on new pipes?

Yes. All our installations carry a workmanship warranty. Material warranties depend on the specific product used — we'll advise you at the time of quote.
