Common Questions

Water Heater FAQs

Tank vs. tankless — which should I choose?

Tank heaters store a fixed volume of pre-heated water and suit larger families with simultaneous demand. Tankless (on-demand) heaters heat water only when needed — more energy-efficient but with a higher upfront cost. We'll recommend the right choice based on your household size and usage patterns.

How often should a water heater be serviced?

Annual servicing is recommended. We flush the tank, inspect the anode rod and pressure relief valve, and check all connections to extend the unit's lifespan and maintain efficiency.

My water heater is making a rumbling noise — what does that mean?

Rumbling or popping is usually caused by sediment buildup at the bottom of the tank. A professional flush typically resolves this. If ignored, it accelerates corrosion and shortens the heater's life.

How long do water heaters last?

Tank water heaters typically last 8–12 years with regular maintenance. Tankless units can last 15–20+ years. If yours is approaching or past these ages, a replacement assessment is worthwhile.

Can you handle solar water heater installations?

Yes. We install solar thermal systems and can also integrate them with electric backup heaters for continuous hot water during overcast days.
